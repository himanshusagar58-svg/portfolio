02 — ABOUT

I know what the software is supposed to feel like from the other side.

I spent time working in café operations before moving seriously into web development. POS screens, cash, payments, staff, customer complaints and the little failures that happen during a busy shift were not abstract problems to me.

That experience shaped the way I build. I care about responsive interfaces, simple flows and software that stays useful when someone is trying to get something done quickly.

I’m still early in my development career. I’m building up my JavaScript and React fundamentals, and I’m looking for a team where I can contribute while getting much better at the parts I’m still learning.
